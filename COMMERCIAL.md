# Commercial Licensing

**Most people do not need this page.** If you are installing Hostzilla on your
own servers — for yourself, your clients, or your company — the
[AGPL-3.0](LICENSE) already covers you, at no cost, with no conditions to meet.
Stop reading and go install it.

## When you do need it

The AGPL has one requirement that matters here: if you modify Hostzilla and let
other people use it over a network, you must offer those users the source of
your modified version.

That is fine for most self-hosters. It is a genuine problem if you want to:

- Offer a hosting product built on a modified Hostzilla without publishing your
  modifications
- Bundle Hostzilla inside a proprietary product you ship to customers
- Build on Hostzilla under a company policy that forbids AGPL code

A commercial licence removes the source-sharing obligation. Everything else
about the software is identical — same code, same repository, same releases.
